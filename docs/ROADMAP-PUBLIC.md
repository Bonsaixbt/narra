# narra — public roadmap

The version for the site and the announcement: no dates, no owners, only what exists and what comes. The dated plan with owners is `ROADMAP.md`.

### Already here

- **The board** — which meta is printing right now: 15m, 60m and 4h windows, HOT / EMERGING / ROTATING / COOLING statuses, narratives, a one-line reading.
- **The coin card** — paste a CA and see whether it sits in a live meta, how many early buyers came from another hot meta, snipers and rotators, its rank inside the meta.
- **Flow** — where repeat buyers moved: every arrow is a list of wallets and transactions, with an hourly flow history.
- **Wallets** — cohorts: snipers, sprayers, rotators, early-in-hot.
- **Trend** — narratives over two days in four-hour steps.
- **Terminal and agents** — `narrahood` on npm: a full-screen terminal, JSON for scripts, an MCP server for Claude, Cursor and any agent.
- **Telegram** — a community bot with a digest and commands, batched alerts every five minutes that link into the site.

### What we are building

- **Extended access for holders** — 15m and 4h windows, full history and the live stream without delay. Switched on a few days after launch, by date.
- **One-call summary and an instant home page** — the first paint of the site from a single response.
- **Alerts and a watchlist** — follow metas and tokens or set your own rules ("tell me when a meta about X turns HOT"), and hear in Telegram when a status changes or wallets leave.
- **Frozen snapshots** — a link to the board as it was when you posted, not as it is now.
- **Share images** — meta and coin cards as PNG, ready for X previews.
- **Sharper narratives** — tokens read from their name, description and links, not only the ticker; AI agents first, because a ticker never says "AI".
- **Published calibration** — what HOT means this week and why; thresholds re-fit from live data and published as data.
- **24h and 7-day boards** — what happened today and this week.
- **Deployer profiles** — farms, serial launchers and the ones whose tokens graduate; the "who is behind this" line on the card.
- **Public track record** — what happened to tokens that were IN a hot meta, 15 and 60 minutes later, as plain numbers, no promises.
- **Social signal** — X mentions next to on-chain money: hype only, chain only, or both.
- **Stock-token metas** — ranked by money, not only by buyers.
- **API keys** — for your own scripts and AI agents, with the same limits holders get.
- **Your own alert rules** — "tell me when a meta with the word X turns HOT", in Telegram and in the stream.
- **More launchpads** — beyond Pons: metas from many launchpads, and waves that run on several at once.
- **Terminal 1.0** — a frozen command contract and versioned schemas, so scripts and agents never break.

### What does not change

- Read-only. No wallet connect, no keys, no buy button.
- Every number opens to its source on chain.
- IN means a token belongs to a live meta. It is not advice.
- The engine is open source; the site adds history and convenience, never secret data.
- Holder features are speed and depth, never a different truth.
